c0004 contracts and reconcile

What changed. Four JSON contracts (snapshot, diff, step, evidence) with a standard-library validator. plan writes a formal diff artifact with per-change reversibility, pointer alignment and a machine-readable verdict. apply is a per-step reconcile loop: reversibility guard, baseline, already_done skip, prechecks, sign-off, one executor call, postcheck against the baseline, gate, one append-only evidence record. Steps and assertions are data; probes answer questions about snapshots. The engine acts only through an Executor interface; SimulatedExecutor is the only implementation and Terraform, Ansible and AWS adapters are named slots that refuse. Irreversible steps are refused without a fresh etcd snapshot. Worker replacement is tiered: canary of one node, then batches of at most 20%.

Why. My 2026 design notes describe the system as a level-triggered reconcile with contracts between layers, declared reversibility and a plane split. c0003 had the gates but not that structure; this makes the structure real and testable.

Verdict. 125 tests green; every snapshot, diff, step and evidence record of a full run validates against its contract; an irreversible step without a snapshot never reaches the executor; 19/19 gate mutations killed; scan OK, 0 private-pattern hits, openspec validate passes.
