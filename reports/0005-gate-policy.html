c0005 gate policy

What changed. --gate manual|assisted|auto. Manual: an operator signature per signoff step, a failed postcheck halts for the operator. Assisted: no signatures, the plan gate reads the diff verdict, each step continues on a passing postcheck and stops on a failing one. Auto: the same decision with nobody present, additionally refusing plans that carry warnings, and allowed only on dev-staging and management clusters. Asking for auto on a workload cluster is refused with exit 2 before a run directory exists. The policy is recorded in state, step definitions and every evidence record and survives resume.

Why. The saving in the design is the jump from a human reading every step to a human woken by exceptions, and that autonomy has to be bounded by cluster class rather than by operator care.

Verdict. 146 tests green; auto refused on prod-class clusters with no run created; assisted completes a prod-class cluster with zero signatures and stops at the failed postcheck on the planted fault; auto runs unattended on dev-1; a fleet with --gate auto pauses at the first prod-class cluster; 23/23 gate mutations killed; scan OK, 0 private-pattern hits, openspec validate passes.
