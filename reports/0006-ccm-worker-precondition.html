c0006 CCM health gates the worker layer

What changed. Every worker-layer step (launch template, canary, each batch) carries a blocking precheck that the cloud-controller-manager is healthy: an unhealthy CCM strands new workers on the uninitialized taint. Fault F2 (prod-south) is re-planted as an old CCM that is healthy at check but broken once the new control plane is up; it now stops at workers:launch_template with the control plane done and no worker replaced. The addon layer still verifies the CCM rollout. The descriptions of F2 in METHOD, README, the testenv README, the fleet ground truth, and the c0002 and c0003 specs and notes are updated; the earlier evidence files and reports carry a note instead of being rewritten.

Why. The failure belongs where it is cheapest to find: before anything is replaced, not after.
