# 0006-ccm-worker-precondition notes

- The open question from c0003 (should CCM health gate the worker layer?) had one right answer once I asked where the failure becomes visible. An unhealthy CCM strands new workers on the `uninitialized` taint. Finding that after every worker is replaced, at the addon layer, is finding it at the most expensive moment. As a precheck it is found before the launch template changes, with nothing replaced.
- It is a dependency edge, not a reordering. Layers stay master, worker, components. What changed is that the worker layer now asserts a fact about a later layer's subject (the CCM) before it starts. Dependencies between layers do not have to follow the layer order.
- The fault had to change shape to be testable. "Rollout unhealthy" only ever fired in the addon layer. The realistic version is "healthy at check, broken once the new control plane is up" (the old CCM does not survive the new apiserver). `check` cannot see it, because check runs before the control plane moves; the worker precheck can. The demo shows `check` COMPLETE on the same cluster that then stops at the worker layer.
- Precheck, not postcheck, is the point: the evidence record of the stop has no `execute` field. The executor was never called, so there is nothing to undo. The older defence (the executor reporting a stuck node) is kept and tested, as defence in depth, but it is the second line.
- Rollback advice for this stop fires no trigger. The control plane is done and healthy, no worker was replaced, no declared trigger holds. The stop is a person's problem to fix (repair or roll the CCM), not a reason to restore etcd.
- Keep the old verification too. The addon layer still checks the CCM rollout, and a purpose-built fleet tests it. Moving one check earlier did not make the later one redundant: they answer different questions (can workers initialise now; did the new CCM version roll out).
- Consistency was the real work of this change. The old stop point was written in the fleet's ground truth, two specs, a proposal, the README, the testenv README, METHOD and two notes. The earlier evidence files and reports are history: annotated with a note, not rewritten.
- A grep for the old claim ("addon layer stops") belongs in acceptance. Contradictory docs are a defect a test can find.
